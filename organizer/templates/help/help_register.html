How to Register

You must register to fully utilize PySawit Web. Registration is a simple, straightforward process:

  1. At the Login page, click on the Register button.

  2. You will be presented with the Register New User page that will receive your profile details. All fields with the exception of your profile photo are mandatory.

  3. Password. Choose a secure password. Your password has to be at least 8 characters in length, and it must contain all of the following:

    1. at least 1 uppercase letter, A-Z
    2. at least 1 lowercase letter, a-z
    3. at least 1 number, 0-9, and
    4. at least 1 symbol, ()[]{}|\`~!@#$%^&*_-+=;:'",<>./?

    For instance, Tsdf-490# is a valid password because it is at least 8 characters in length, has at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one symbol.

  4. Email. Your email is very important because communications between PySawit Web and you will be through your email. Requests for account activation and password reset, for instance, are done through email. This includes Wiki notifications.

  5. Profile photo. Your photo is optional, but if you choose to have one, you need to upload a photo file no larger than 0.5 Mb. Your profile photo will also be resized to no more than 200 pixels wide and tall.

    To upload your photo, click on the Browse button (Fig. 2) to find and select your photo file. This file will only be uploaded and appear in your profile page after you have submitted your registration form.

    If you have a current profile photo, it will be replaced by the new photo. The old photo file will be deleted and cannot be recovered. Note that you can also choose to have no profile photo by placing a check mark (tick) in the Remove profile photo box.

  6. Once all your details are filled in, tick the reCAPTCHA verification box, then click on the Create New Account button.

  7. A verification email will be immediately sent to your mailbox. Inside this email is a link which you will have to click to verify your identity. Upon verification, your registration is complete, and you will be able to fully use PySawit Web.

    Note: You may have to check your email spam box for the email from PySawit Web.

  8. Should you lose your account activation email, you can request that an email be sent to you again by clicking on the Lost activation email? link (Fig. 2).

    Similarly, should you forget your password, you can request for a password reset by clicking on the Forgot password? link (Fig. 1).
