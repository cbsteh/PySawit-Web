Insert Images

You may also have images in your Wiki articles. You need to upload your images and insert them into the article. Open your article you wish to edit (Article 1.3 in this example), and click on the Article menu and Edit option to edit the article.

  1. Click on the Images panel near the bottom of the page.

  2. This will open the Images panel to reveal the option to upload your images. Click on the Choose File button (Step 1, Fig. 3), after which a dialog box will appear where you will need to find and select your image file (in this example, the image file to upload is demo.png) in your local computer (Step 2 and 3, Fig. 4). Once selected, click on the Add Image button (Step 4, Fig. 3) to upload your file.

  3. The previous step only uploads your image file, but it is not yet inserted in your article. To do so, place your cursor at the location where you want the uploaded image to appear in your article. In this example, the image will be placed at the end of the article, so navigate to the end of the article, and place the cursor at the desired insertion point.

    Now open the Images panel again, and click on the Insert link of the image you wish to insert.

  4. An Insert Image dialog box will appear prompting you for a few options before the selected image is inserted. The first is the Alignment option. Decide if you wish to have the image aligned to the left, right, or center.

    Next, for the image Size field, decide if the inserted image should appear small, medium, large, or original size in the article. You can also leave the size to default for PySawit Web to decide on the appropriate image size to show.

    Enter a suitable caption for the image in the Caption field, after which you can click on the Insert image button.

  5. The image is now inserted in your article (Fig. 7) via the following syntax: 

      [image:2]
      Demo inserted picture

    where [image:2] indicates the image file with the id 2 (see Fig. 5). Every image file has a unique id, and the file that was just uploaded, demo.png, was assigned the id 2. Next is the line below the image, Demo inserted picture which is the image caption, and a caption must be indented by four spaces.

  6. Once saved, the article should appear like that in Fig. 8.

  7. You can easily modify the specifications of the inserted image by editing its image syntax. In Fig. 7, the inserted image syntax is: 

      [image:2]
      Demo inserted picture

    which can be modified to: 

      [image:2 align:right size:large]
      Demo inserted picture

    to make the image appear instead in large size and aligned to the right. Likewise, 

      [image:2 align:left size:small]
      Demo inserted picture

    will make the image appear in small size and aligned to the left. Note that if the fields align and size are omitted, like in the case in Fig. 7, then the image will appear in default size and aligned center.
