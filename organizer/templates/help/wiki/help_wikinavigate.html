Navigating Wiki

Wiki is a collection of articles. Very similar Wikipedia, Wiki enables group collaboration to prepare articles. All users work on a single copy of an article, where they each can edit or revise the article. An article can also be rewound to a previous, old copy to reset the latest changes to the article.

  1. All articles are descendants of the root article. There can only be one root article. The root article will often contain instructions to users on the group project or work. The root article also cannot be changed or deleted as it is owned by the admin of PySawit Web who will block any modifications to this article. Also note that some articles may also be blocked by the admin to prevent modifications by users.

    Click on the Browse link which will bring up a side menu or side bar showing the hierarchy of all articles in the Wiki.

  2. The side menu lists all articles and their hierarchy in Wiki. To access an article, you only have to click on the article's name on the list. A check mark appears next to the currently viewed article (in this example, the root article is the current article being viewed).

  3. All articles will be displayed in a structure shown in Fig. 3. The breadcrumbs at the top the page shows the hierarchy of the current article. In Fig. 3, the article being viewed is named Article 1.1 (the article name can be changed), and according to the breadcrumbs, this article is directly under Article 1, which in turn is under the root article. You can verify this hierarchy by looking at the list of articles in Fig. 2. You can also click on any article in the breadcrumbs to access it.

    Slug is a name given to the link to access the article. You do not have give a name to the slug. If none is provided by you, then one will be given by PySawit Web by default. The Article menu will list a series of options to create, edit, and move articles.
