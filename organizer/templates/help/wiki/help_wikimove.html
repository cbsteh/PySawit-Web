Move Articles

Articles can be easily moved to another location, but any links to the article being moved will not be updated. For instance, Article A has a link to Article B, and if Article B is moved to another location, the link in Article A will still point to the old location of Article B. Consequently, such links must be manually updated.

In this example, Article 1.3 will be moved from its current location (under Article 1) to a new location under Article 2, so that Article 1.3 is now a child or descendant article of Article 2.

The following describes how to move an article:

  1. Navigate to the article you want to move (Article 1.3 in this example), and open it for editing (see here for instructions on editing articles). Click on the Move button.

  2. Select the article's move destination in the Destination dropdown list, then click on the Move article button.

  3. The article is now located at the intended new location. Recall that any links to Article 1.3 will not be updated.
