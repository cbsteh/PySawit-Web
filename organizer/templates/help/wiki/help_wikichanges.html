View Changes in Articles

A history of all revisions to an article are kept, so that you are able to see all the changes that were ever made to a given article. You can roll back the article's latest changes (that is, undo the current changes) to a past version, and you may also merge the current article with a selected past version (that is, combine two versions of the article).

The following describes how to view an article's past revisions, undo the latest changes, and merge two article versions:

  1. To view the history of changes to an article, you first need to open the selected article for editing (see here for instructions on editing articles). Then click on the Article menu, and select the Changes option.

  2. You will be presented with a complete list of past revisions of the article, where the latest changes are listed first, followed by subsequently older changes the farther down the list. In Fig. 2, there have been a total of six revisions to the article, where the first revision (revision #1, the last in the list) indicates the article's creation. We can see that this article was created by user admin (the site administrator) on Nov. 25, 2018 at 2:45 pm. Since then, most of the article's changes were done by user test4 (revisions #2 to #5), and the latest changes (revision #6) were done by admin on Nov. 27, 2018 at 6:00 pm.

  3. Note: you can view the past changes of the article by clicking on the preview this revision button on the selected article version (Fig. 2). For instance, if you wish to see how the article looked like during revision #4, you can click on the preview this revision button for revision #4.

    Alternatively, you can also click on the the article revision title. Try clicking on the revision title for revision #4. It will reveal what were added (in green) or deleted (in red) from the article during this stage.

    Similarly, now try clicking on the revision title for revision #6 to see exactly what has changed in the latest article version. Fig. 4 reveals that user admin had only added one line in the article at line no. 81. This latest revision also revealed that nothing was deleted from the article (nothing was indicated in red).

  4. To roll back (undo) the latest changes, select the desired article version, then click on the Switch to selected version button. This will roll back the article to the selected past version.

  5. You can also merge the latest article version with one from a past version. To do this, select the desired past version, then click on the Merge with selected version button. This will combine both versions into one.
