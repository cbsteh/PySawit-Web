Create Articles

Whenever you create an article, you must first decide where to place this new article. Let's say that you want to add a new article named Article 1.3 and have it placed under the Article 1 article (parent), like shown in Fig. 1, where this new article is also at the same level as Article 1.1 and Article 1.2 (siblings).

  1. There are two ways to add Article 1.3. The first is to navigate to one of its (future) sibling articles, Article 1.1 (you can also choose Article 1.2). Now, click on the Article menu, then select the New article on same level option. We choose this option because this new article will be on the same level as the current article.

  2. Alternatively, the second method is to navigate to its (future) parent article, Article 1 (Fig. 3). Now, click on the Article menu, then select the New article on a level below option (Fig. 4). We choose this option because this new article will be one level below the current article.

  3. You will next be presented with the Add new article page. Type in the article name (in this example, the name will be Article 1.3) in the Title field. The slug field refers to the name of the link for this article. If you do not want to give it a name, just leave this field to PySawit Web to provide it with a name.

    The Contents field is where you will enter in the article contents (see here for instructions for this). The last field is Summary in which you will enter a brief description of this article (such as the reason for its creation or current changes). This field is optional and can be left blank.

    Click on the Create article button to save your entries and complete the creation of this new article.

  4. Your new article will be presented like in Fig. 6, and you can verify its position in the hierarchy through the article's breadcrumbs or the list of articles (Fig. 7).
