Article Settings

Article settings refer to notifications that you can choose to receive whenever a watched article is being changed. Notifications can either be through email, when you are logged in, or both. Notifications are useful when you want to be informed whenever a watched article is changed by other users. Without notifications, you would have to frequently check if the article has been changed.

The following explains about setting the article notification:

  1. Navigate to the article you want to watch or monitor of any changes from other users. In this example, Article 1.3 will be watched for changes. Open this article for editing (see here for instructions on editing articles), and from the Article menu, choose the Settings option.

  2. In the Notification page, place a check mark (tick) whether you want to receive notifications whenever you are logged in or by email. You can opt to have both types of notifications by putting a check mark on each of these two boxes.

  3. If you choose to receive notifications whenever you are logged in, a notification count badge (e.g., 1, 2, etc.) will appear next to your username on the main menu. This badge will contain the number of notifications. In Fig. 3, there is one notification because only one article, Article 1.3, has been changed. Click on the article's name on the menu to quickly navigate to the watched article.

  4. If you are watching many articles, you may receive many notifications, especially if you have chosen notifications by email. To keep your notifications by email more manageable, you can change the frequency of receiving such emails.

    On the main menu, click on your username menu. Now click on the Notification settings option.

  5. Choose the interval for receiving email notifications in the Interval dropdown list. Choose instantly to receive emails immediately after each time watched article is changed. This option can result in many emails being sent to you in a day. The daily option means only one email per watched article is sent once a day to you even though a watched article may change several times in a day. The last option is weekly which works like the daily option but the email is sent to you only once a week.

    You can fine-tune the notifications for each watched article by selecting the watched articles from the Remove subscriptions list. Click on the No emails radio button and then the Save button to stop all email notifications for the selected articles. Likewise, click on the Email on any change radio button, then the Save button will start instantaneous email notifications whenever the selected watched articles are changed.
