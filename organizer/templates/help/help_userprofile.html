User Profile

Your profile details such as username and profile photo can be changed.

  1. Your user profile details are accessed via your User menu.

  2. View profile. Click on the View my profile to view your profile details. This page also lists all your run files, weather files, and Wiki articles that you have created.

  3. Edit profile. To change your personal details, click on the Edit button (Fig.2), or click on the Edit my profile option on the User menu (Fig. 1).

      See here on details about changing or removing your profile photo.

  4. Change password. You cannot change your password via your profile page. To change your current password, go to the User menu, and select the Change my password option (Fig. 1), which will lead you to a page (Fig. 4) to enter your current then new password.

  5. Forgot password. You can reset your password if you have forgotten your password. At the Login page, click on the Forgot password? link. Now enter the same email address you had used during registration because an email containing a password reset link will be sent to you at this email address.
